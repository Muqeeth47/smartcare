01 • CHALLENGE ASSESSMENT

The Problem: Fragmentation & Stock-Outs in Public Health

Public healthcare systems across India face persistent supply chain vulnerabilities, delayed paper telemetry, and inability to dynamically respond during health emergencies.

Systemic Healthcare Bottlenecks

Opaque Rural Inventories

Clinics experience unrecorded stock-outs of life-saving medicines (antipyretics, snake antivenom, antibiotics) leading to patient turnaround.

Unpredicted Bed & Oxygen Telemetry

No real-time status of ICU, Oxygen, and General Ward bed occupancy during sudden regional disease outbreaks.

Clinical Attendance Asymmetry

Doctor and nurse shift attendance untracked, causing dangerous Patient:Doctor ratio spikes (>45:1) without administrative awareness.

The SmartCare Solution

Automated 15-Minute Telemetry Sync

Continuous edge telemetry automatically updates medicine stock levels, bed turnover, and medical personnel shifts on state dashboards.

AI Epidemic Surge Forecaster

Simulates seasonal outbreak spikes (Monsoon Dengue, Gastroenteritis, Viral Flu) to predict depletion dates 72 hours in advance.

Multi-State Federated AI (FedAvg)

Aggregates model weights across 5 states without moving citizen health records, guaranteeing zero patient PII exposure under the DPDP Act.

07 • NATIONAL FEDERATED AI MESH

Multi-State Federated AI (FedAvg): Privacy by Design

Collaborative national machine learning across 5 Indian states without centralizing or exposing sensitive patient health records.

5 State Nodes • FedAvg Gradient Synthesis • Global Accuracy 98.7% • Zero PII Exposed

States (Telangana, Maharashtra, Kerala, UP, Karnataka) compute local gradients. Only encrypted model weights are exchanged.

Guarantees absolute compliance with India's DPDP Act 2023 and ABDM standards. Patient health records never leave state borders.

The shared national model achieves 98.7% prediction accuracy with global loss at 0.038, enabling nationwide outbreak preparedness.
