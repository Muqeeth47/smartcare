07 • National Federated AI Mesh
Multi-State FedAvg: Privacy by Design
Collaborative national ML across 5 Indian states — patient health records never leave state borders.
smartcare-omega-two.vercel.app / state / federated
5 State Nodes • FedAvg Synthesis • 98.7% Global Accuracy • Zero PII Exposed
Federated Averaging (FedAvg)
Telangana, Maharashtra, Kerala, UP, Karnataka each compute local gradients on private data. Only encrypted model weights are exchanged — never raw patient records.
Zero Patient PII Exposure
Full compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and ABDM standards. Health records never cross state administrative boundaries.
98.7% National Model Accuracy
Global loss at 0.038 — the shared model enables nationwide epidemic outbreak preparedness weeks before seasonal demand peaks hit clinic shelves.
Google Vertex AI Orchestration
State-level model training scheduled, aggregated, and deployed via Vertex AI pipelines — scalable to all 36 Indian states and UTs.
